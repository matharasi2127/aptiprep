Number System

Important formulas and shortcuts

Divisibility by 2

Last digit is 0, 2, 4, 6 or 8

If the last digit is even, the number is divisible by 2.

Divisibility by 3

Sum of digits is divisible by 3

Example: 123 → 1 + 2 + 3 = 6.

Divisibility by 5

Last digit = 0 or 5

A number ending in 0 or 5 is divisible by 5.

LCM × HCF

LCM × HCF = Product of two numbers

This relation is useful for two positive integers.

Number of Factors

n = pᵃ × qᵇ → Factors = (a+1)(b+1)

Used when a number is expressed as prime factors.

Sum of First n Natural Numbers

n(n + 1) / 2

Example: 1 + 2 + 3 + ... + n.

Percentages

Common percentage formulas

Percentage

Percentage = (Part / Total) × 100

Basic formula for calculating percentage.

Percentage Increase

Increase % = (Increase / Original) × 100

Increase = New value − Original value.

Percentage Decrease

Decrease % = (Decrease / Original) × 100

Decrease = Original value − New value.

Successive Changes

Net % = a + b + (ab / 100)

For two successive increases. Use the signs of a and b for increase/decrease.

Fraction to Percentage

Fraction × 100 = Percentage

Example: 1/4 × 100 = 25%.

Profit & Loss

Business mathematics formulas

Profit

Profit = SP − CP

When selling price is greater than cost price.

Loss

Loss = CP − SP

When cost price is greater than selling price.

Profit Percentage

Profit % = (Profit / CP) × 100

Profit percentage is calculated on cost price.

Loss Percentage

Loss % = (Loss / CP) × 100

Loss percentage is calculated on cost price.

Discount

Discount = MP − SP

MP = Marked Price and SP = Selling Price.

Discount Percentage

Discount % = (Discount / MP) × 100

Discount percentage is calculated on marked price.

Ratio & Proportion

Ratio-based aptitude formulas

Basic Ratio

a : b = a / b

Ratio compares two quantities of the same kind.

Equivalent Ratio

a:b = ka:kb

Multiply or divide both terms by the same non-zero number.

Proportion

a:b = c:d → ad = bc

Product of extremes equals product of means.

Direct Proportion

x₁/y₁ = x₂/y₂

When one quantity increases, the other increases proportionally.

Averages

Average and weighted average formulas

Basic Average

Average = Sum of values / Number of values

The most commonly used average formula.

Sum from Average

Sum = Average × Number of values

Useful for finding missing totals.

Weighted Average

Weighted Average = Σ(wx) / Σw

Used when values have different weights.

Time, Speed & Distance

Travel and motion formulas

Speed

Speed = Distance / Time

Basic relationship between speed, distance and time.

Distance

Distance = Speed × Time

Use when speed and time are known.

Time

Time = Distance / Speed

Use when distance and speed are known.

Average Speed

Average Speed = Total Distance / Total Time

Do not simply average speeds unless conditions allow it.

Relative Speed

Same direction → |S₁ − S₂|

For opposite directions, relative speed is S₁ + S₂.

km/hr to m/s

km/hr × 5/18 = m/s

Example: 36 km/hr = 10 m/s.

Time & Work

Work and efficiency formulas

Work Rate

Work Rate = 1 / Time

If a person completes work in x days, daily work = 1/x.

Combined Work

Combined Rate = 1/x + 1/y

Used when two people work together.

Efficiency

Efficiency ∝ 1 / Time

More efficiency means less time for the same work.

Work Formula

Work = Efficiency × Time

Useful for comparing workers.

Simple & Compound Interest

Banking and interest formulas

Simple Interest

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

P = Principal, R = Rate, T = Time.

Amount

Amount = Principal + Interest

Total amount after adding interest.

Compound Amount

A = P(1 + R/100)ᵀ

Standard compound interest formula for annual compounding.

Compound Interest

CI = A − P

Compound interest equals amount minus principal.

Algebra

Important algebraic identities

Identity 1

(a + b)² = a² + 2ab + b²

Basic square identity.

Identity 2

(a − b)² = a² − 2ab + b²

Useful for quick simplification.

Difference of Squares

a² − b² = (a+b)(a−b)

Common factorization identity.

Cube Identity

a³ + b³ = (a+b)(a²−ab+b²)

Useful for factorization.

Permutation & Combination

Arrangement and selection formulas

Factorial

n! = n × (n−1) × ... × 1

Example: 5! = 120.

Permutation

ⁿPᵣ = n! / (n−r)!

Used when order matters.

Combination

ⁿCᵣ = n! / [r!(n−r)!]

Used when order does not matter.

Probability

Basic probability formulas

Basic Probability

P(E) = Favourable Outcomes / Total Outcomes

Used when outcomes are equally likely.

Complement

P(not E) = 1 − P(E)

Probability of an event not occurring.

Probability Range

0 ≤ P(E) ≤ 1

Probability always lies between 0 and 1.

Mensuration

Area, perimeter and volume formulas

Rectangle Area

Area = Length × Breadth

Area of a rectangle.

Rectangle Perimeter

Perimeter = 2(L + B)

L = Length, B = Breadth.

Square Area

Area = Side²

Area of a square.

Circle Area

Area = πr²

r = radius.

Circle Circumference

Circumference = 2πr

Perimeter of a circle.

Cuboid Volume

Volume = L × B × H

L = Length, B = Breadth, H = Height.

Cylinder Volume

Volume = πr²h

r = radius and h = height.

Important geometry concepts

Basic triangle area formula.

Triangle Angle Sum

A + B + C = 180°

Sum of interior angles of a triangle.

Right Triangle

Hypotenuse² = Base² + Height²

Pythagorean theorem.

Quadrilateral Angle Sum

Sum of interior angles of a quadrilateral.

Clocks & Calendars

Important clock and calendar formulas

Clock Angle

Angle = |30H − 5.5M|

H = hour and M = minutes.

Minute Hand

6° per minute

The minute hand moves 6 degrees every minute.

Hour Hand

0.5° per minute

The hour hand moves half a degree every minute.

Leap Year

Divisible by 4, except century years unless divisible by 400

Example: 2000 is a leap year; 1900 is not.
